Abstract. The AI Scientist-v2 is an end-to-end agentic system that produced the first entirely AI-generated peer-review-accepted workshop paper. Versus v1 it removes human-authored code templates, generalizes across ML domains, and uses progressive agentic tree search managed by an experiment manager, plus a VLM feedback loop for figures. One of three ICLR workshop submissions exceeded the average human acceptance threshold. Code: https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2.
