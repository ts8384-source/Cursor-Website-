Documents the April 2026 cluster of wiki-style memory proposals (Karpathy LLM Wiki, MemPalace, LLM Wiki v2) against RAG and production memory (MemGPT, Mem0, Zep, A-Mem, MemMachine, SleepGate, Second Me).
