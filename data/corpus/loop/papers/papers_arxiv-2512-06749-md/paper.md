Abstract. Log-only failure attribution in multi-agent systems is unreliable because multiple distinct interventions can independently repair the same failure. DoVer augments hypothesis generation with active verification (editing messages, altering plans, rerunning). On Magnetic-One / GAIA and AssistantBench, DoVer flips 18–28% of failed trials into successes and validates or refutes 30–60% of hypotheses. ICLR 2026. https://aka.ms/DoVer
