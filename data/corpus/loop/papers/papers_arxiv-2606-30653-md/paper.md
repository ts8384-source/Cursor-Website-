Abstract. Measures generator-evaluator self-consistency across 10 frontier models on 491 concepts. Higher self-consistency correlates with higher vulnerability to physician-validated mistakes. Same-model self-review tends to approve its own blind spots; diverse ensembles outperform N copies of the same agent. This is the consistency-dilemma result for evaluator/voting agents.
